Ligue 1 Match Predictor

MACHINE LEARNING

Can a model call a French football match before kick-off? I merged 8 data files (more than 10 seasons of matches, player market values, lineups, cards) into one clean table, built new features such as the budget gap and each team's recent form, and trained three classifiers to predict home win, draw or away win for the 2025 season.

  • Rolling "last 5 matches" features, shifted by one game so the model never sees the result it predicts
  • Split by time: trained up to 2022, tested on 2023–24
  • 52% accuracy vs a 44% baseline; feature selection kept almost the same score with only 3 variables

Network Coding Research (MIT)

RESEARCH · NETWORKS

Six-week research internship in Muriel Médard's Network Coding and Reliable Communications Group at MIT, with three classmates. After simulating noisy channels in Python, I worked on Random Linear Network Coding for the SNOB 5G project: packets are mixed with random coefficients, sent over a lossy channel, and rebuilt by inverting a matrix as soon as enough of them arrive.

  • Added retransmissions, then measured throughput and delay over 40,000 generations for different loss rates
  • Derived the theoretical delay with binomial probabilities: it matched our simulations
  • Presented our results to Prof. Médard

Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe AI

GAME AI

An AI for Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe, a 9×9 version where each move decides which small board your opponent has to play in next. It uses minimax with alpha-beta pruning and iterative deepening: it searches deeper and deeper until its time is up, and keeps the best move found so far. The demo is a JavaScript port of my Python code.

  • Transposition table so positions already analysed are never searched twice
  • Killer-move and history heuristics to try the most promising moves first
  • Hand-tuned evaluation: won boards, two-in-a-rows, control of the centre

Liv'in Paris

GRAPHS · DATABASES

A C# platform where home cooks sell meals to people in Paris, with a MySQL database for users, dishes and orders. Deliveries follow the real metro network: we modelled every station as a graph and implemented Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford and Floyd-Warshall to find the fastest route between cook and customer.

  • Graph tools: connectivity, cycle detection, Welsh-Powell colouring
  • Statistics: best customers, deliveries per cook, average order price
  • WinForms visualisation, JSON/XML export, unit tests with MSTest

Tipping Behaviour Analysis

STATISTICS

Descriptive statistics on a survey of 709 people in Norway about restaurant tips. With two classmates, I studied one variable of each type (binary, ordinal, discrete, continuous): frequency tables, quartiles, variance, box plots, ECDF, and outlier detection with the IQR rule. The demo uses our real results.

  • Response time: mean 148 s but median 105 s, a strongly skewed variable
  • 48 outliers found with the 1.5 × IQR rule, shown on a log scale

Earthquake-Resistant Platform

EMBEDDED

Year-long project in a team of six: a model building that stays level during an earthquake. A laser-cut box holds the Arduino and the batteries; above it, two servo motors stacked at 90° correct the pitch and the roll measured by an MPU6050 gyroscope, keeping the LED panel and the 3D-printed tower level. Parts are wood and recycled plastic.

  • Sensor reading and real-time correction loop programmed on the Arduino
  • CAD, machining, electronics and 3D printing, all done by the team

Alterdune, a C++ RPG

C++ · OOP

A turn-based console RPG in C++, inspired by Undertale: in each fight you can attack, use an item, or calm the monster down with actions until you are allowed to spare it. Monsters and items are loaded from CSV files, and the code is built around classes and inheritance (normal monsters, mini-bosses, bosses) with manual memory management.

Hexapod Robot

ROBOTICS

Year-long project in a team of six: a six-legged robot inspired by insects, driven by only two electric motors. We designed the whole robot in SolidWorks, worked out the gear ratios to get the right balance between speed and torque, and cut every wooden part with a laser cutter.

  • Each leg is moved by a crank and slides in a slot, so a simple rotation becomes a walking step
  • Legs move in two groups of three (tripod gait), so the robot always stands on three feet
